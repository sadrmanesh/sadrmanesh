### About

I'm an AI product engineer and founder who takes AI from idea to production. I work where models meet real users: shaping the product, designing the system, and shipping software that people rely on every day.

My focus is simple: AI should be judged by the problems it solves, not by the demo it makes.

### What I focus on

- **AI & LLM products:** turning large language models into reliable, shipped products with clear user value
- **Agentic workflows & developer tooling:** agents, skills, rules and context systems that make engineers and teams faster
- **Strategy to production:** from product discovery and roadmap to architecture, launch and iteration
- **E-commerce & marketplaces:** search, discovery and recommendation for buyers and sellers
- **Tech leadership & digital transformation:** helping teams and businesses adopt AI in ways that last
- **Open source:** building in public and sharing the tools behind the products
